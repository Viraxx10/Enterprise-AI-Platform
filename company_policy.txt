SECTION 1: CUSTOMER ONBOARDING AND ACCOUNT VERIFICATION
All newly registered enterprise clients must complete Level-2 identity 
verification within 14 calendar days of account provisioning. If verification 
documents (such as corporate registration certificates and tax identification numbers) 
are not validated within this window, the account is placed in "Restricted Tier" 
with an outbound transfer cap of $5,000 USD per transaction.

SECTION 2: DORMANT ACCOUNTS AND RETENTION ESCALATION
Accounts showing zero user logins or transaction activity for 90 consecutive days 
are classified as "Inactive". 
1. Day 91: The system triggers an automated 3-part re-engagement email sequence offering a dedicated account manager review.
2. Day 120: If no engagement occurs, monthly maintenance fees are suspended to protect user equity, and API access tokens are temporarily revoked.
3. Day 180: Accounts with balances under $100 are scheduled for automatic closure following a final 30-day written notification via registered email.

SECTION 3: DATA RETENTION AND PURGE COMPLIANCE
User audit logs, transaction telemetry, and API query records must be preserved 
in cold storage for exactly 7 years to meet global financial compliance standards. 
After the 7-year mark, automated cryptographic erasure scripts permanently delete 
the telemetry data from primary and backup clusters. Customers invoking the "Right 
to be Forgotten" (GDPR Article 17) will have personal identifiable information (PII) 
scrubbed within 30 business days, excluding immutable ledger transactions.

SECTION 4: SLA GUARANTEES AND INCIDENT REPORTING
The platform maintains an infrastructure uptime target of 99.95% excluding scheduled 
weekend maintenance windows. For P1 (Critical Outage) incidents:
- The incident response team must acknowledge alerts within 15 minutes.
- Status updates must be posted to the public trust dashboard every 30 minutes.
- Root Cause Analysis (RCA) documents must be delivered to enterprise tier customers within 5 business days of incident resolution.
